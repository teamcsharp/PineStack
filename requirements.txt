# The Library's PDF reader (#1158). The shelf at //10.89.1.125/QuickSwap/Manuals
# is mostly PDF, and this image has no poppler - pdftotext lives on the HOST,
# not in python:3.12-slim - so without this line every PDF on the shelf is
# unreadable and library_extract raises "no pdf reader on this box".
# Measured against poppler on all seven manuals before it was chosen: word
# recall is 100% (the raw CHARACTER counts look half as big only because
# `pdftotext -layout` pads with spaces to hold the page geometry), and pypdf
# actually finds MORE unique words - it picks up diagram labels poppler drops.
# Pure python, py3-none-any, no compiler and no system libraries, which is
# what makes it the right answer on aarch64. EPUB, zip, docx and plain text
# need nothing at all beyond the stdlib.
