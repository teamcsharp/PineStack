The actual network folder is `\\10.89.1.125\QuickSwap\samples_grabbed`,
not the requested `samples\_grabbed` spelling. Its existing read-only mount
contains 15,761 MP3 files across 11 immediate child collections. Discovery uses
rotating subsets and prioritizes fresh arrivals; it rescans every 60 seconds.
The duration cache now retains up to 32,000 readings instead of clearing itself
at 8,000 entries. This corrects the cache behavior; no throughput gain is claimed
from an unmeasured live benchmark.

The previous routing watcher skipped finished recordings whenever the floor was
busy. Continuous talk could therefore starve that queue. It now joins the fair
speech queue and rechecks the route after waiting. Re-selecting Nabu immediately
updates the desktop and schedules a coalesced wake instead of requiring a full
device initialization. Explicit output-off choices remain respected.

Nabu has separate music and announcement pipelines but a shared physical volume.
The new controls adjust cached audio derivatives, leaving the original recordings
intact. The master dial is not a per-stream control. Already buffered speech keeps
its prior level until that clip ends. Music commands have their own short lock,
so muting music does not wait for a long DJ announcement. Route, track and level
checks prevent an obsolete queued conversion from restarting music.

SFX cadence counts recorded host replies/segments; it does not split an existing
multi-sentence recording into artificial timing points. Optional effects and
character turns yield when the broadcast slot is tight. The character's off-air
bank applies the selected Crystal and rhyme requirements before recording. No
model or voice synthesis runs in the strict playback path. Muted hardware can
complete delivery without being credited as heard; independent audible page
receipts remain eligible and are deduplicated.

The first production observation exposed two additional blockers. The sample pool
remained empty until a complete NAS scan finished about 366 seconds after restart,
despite thousands of cached short clips. Warm startup now publishes a bounded,
verified subset before the complete scan, then adds candidates progressively.
Exact modification-time, duration, folder and settings checks protect publication;
periodic scans retain existing variety until replacement results are ready.

The SFX writer also repeatedly failed to enter the busy tint category. When fewer
than two distinct generic replies are actually available, it now gets one bounded
admission slot in the same model FIFO and global gate. Reserved takes and recently
heard wording do not falsely count as available stock. Model choice, rhyme grading
and voice proof remain unchanged. Once the reserve is sufficient, ordinary tint
admission applies. This does not bypass older saved audio waiting to play.
