Repeated declines had repairable causes: an unfinished caller monopolized retries, cleanup erased bar boundaries, pronunciation checks missed rhymes, control responses were treated as prose, and learning repeated advice without measuring outcomes. The fixes preserve original dialogue and technical checks; they do not prove every newly accepted rewrite faithful.

The consistent snapshot covers events **3538–5433**, approximately nine hours: **1,896 occurrences**, **1,192 distinct source/candidate pairs**, and **620 gate/kind/source families**. Of those events, **283 are cuts across 183 distinct review IDs**; 1,493 are rejected rewrites, 78 record trimming, and 42 are held before recording. Thus the 183-cut notification and the larger rejection history describe different things. Neither is a station-wide failure rate. Current pending storage at that snapshot was 2,834 records. Full wording, evaluations, occurrence identities, snapshot provenance and trace references remain in the [corpus audit](rejection-learning-followup-audit.json).

Tint accounts for 1,534 events. Other gates include draft trimming 78, segment brief 75, draft fragment 65, caller contract 43, radio draft 31 and blend 30. Only 15 events are marked technical. Rhyme appears in 1,184 events, semantic preservation in 391 and transformation in 198; reasons overlap. Non-tint rows retain text but generally lack exact model-trace pointers; nearby unrelated calls are not assigned to them. [Gate analysis](rejection-learning-followup-audit.md).

The strongest retry example is “Just get to the point, man. We got records to spin.” It generated 446 events and 53 candidate variants. One parent contributed 439 events across 115 trace scopes over 6.44 hours while holding 20 accepted turns out of 21. The strike limit applied only to completed scripts, never bounding this unfinished turn. Its explicit operator acceptance must remain respected.

The new durable retry budget counts actual model responses without increased accepted coverage. Four such responses yield for five minutes, then ten, twenty and at most thirty minutes. Admission deferral without model work costs nothing. Originals, accepted turns, failed candidates, voices and operator decisions remain stored; selectors can choose another eligible parent. Changed relevant source/profile/prompt/recipe or explicit reviewer recovery releases the wait. An unrelated road's learning revision does not. Whole-only repair now regrades retained work, requests one bounded group of failed IDs, supplies accepted neighbours as context, and preserves progress on cancellation or deferral. Unchanged refusals retain their review references. [Implementation and isolated regressions](rejection-learning-followup-audit.md).

Bar cleanup changes slashes into semicolon pauses, preserving pairs such as “I stay near / Have no fear.” The provider adds pronunciation evidence from a pinned, bundled [CMUdict](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict) snapshot without runtime downloads. It compares actual endings from the final stressed vowel and retains pronunciation/version evidence. Repeated “man” tags and shared unstressed suffixes supply no new proof. The [v8 replay](crystal-cmu-v8-replay.json) compares identical inputs with the provider disabled/enabled: 1,529 rejected occurrences, 880 distinct pairs, 223 additional rhyme passes and 209 additional effective passes across 121 pairs. Fourteen newly rhyming occurrences still fail semantics. These are retained-event comparisons, not independent generation trials. [Safeguards and provenance](crystal-cmu-evidence.md).

Upstream fixes are deliberately specific. Track reception may return its exact `NONE` sentinel without ordinary prose validation rejecting it. Known punctuation-repair instruction echoes are removed before generic trimming, with the untouched raw response retained in the trace. Positive English context resolves the observed short “Yo” and “die trying” refusals while foreign-language counterexamples remain rejected. Impersonal “make one wonder” and indefinite “another one” no longer invent numeric obligations. Genuine missing products, sales, speaker structure or protected passages remain failures: the mug/lighthouse ads and medical-story blend in the audit should not pass merely because their wording rhymes.

The former learner had twelve permanent incumbent hints and no measured success feedback. News and SFX Guy received no learned guidance despite repeated eligible failures. It now allocates up to four hints per kind, retains the latest source-specific evidence, and records actual request/turn identities, used recipes, prior attempts, model/profile/version cohorts and raw/effective grades. Technical, deferred, preview and cached regrade work do not train it. Three bounded procedures per fault family can be explored, supported by comparable observed outcomes, or exhausted. Promotion requires better target/effective results without worse factual or rhyme failures; repeated retries of one source are not independent support. Rollback pauses adaptation. This is measured selection among explicit recipes, not an extra LLM generating prompts. [Learner audit and validation](prompt-learning-effectiveness-followup.md).
