## What was wrong, as measured on the live station

1. **SFX database: clips would not play.** The clip book kept `playable=1`
   for files gone from the share (about 4% of a sample). `/sfx/{id}` returned
   404 before its quarantine ran, so nothing ever stood them down.
2. **No DJs at 100% talk.** Three layers stopped every finished round:
   - the playout sequencer was in `linear` with 304 queued asks and 1,488
     stamps inside sold air, so `_ready_round_fits` put every round's start
     after its slot's deadline. **The mode file is now `shadow`**
     (`/app/data/playout/mode`); writing `linear` back re-enables it.
   - with linear off, the silence rescue still aired nothing: 53 of 55 walks
     picked an advert (139 unheard, the oldest stock) and the booth refused
     each one, with no reason recorded. The next-oldest ad was picked every time.
   - at 100% talk `dj_banter` refuses live writing while the larder is empty,
     so nothing else filled the gap.

## What changed

| Tag | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| `[sfx-gone]` | `sfx_library.py`, `app.py` `/sfx` | Shown rows are checked against the share off the loop. A gone file reads "file missing", gets no player, and is quarantined. An unmounted share is never called gone. |
| `[sfx-switch]` | `filemgr.js`, `sfx-db.js` (desktop + pine-views) | A header button switches File management to the SFX database and back. |
| `[door-why]` | `app.py` | A scheduled road that airs nothing logs its cupboard's reason. Every bare `return []` in `_speak_turns_floorless`/`_banter_air` names itself through `_banter_no`. The cupboard desk no longer says "nothing is wrong" about a row `dialogue_row_ready` refuses. |
| `[road-backoff]` | `app.py` `unheard_pick` | Two booth refusals in a row and a road sits out the silence rescue for 10 min (`PINE_UNHEARD_ROAD_REST`). An airing puts it back. |
