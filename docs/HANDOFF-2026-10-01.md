## What was wrong, as measured on the live station

1. **SFX database: clips would not play.** The clip book kept `playable=1` for
   files gone from the share (about 4% of a sample). `/sfx/{id}` returned 404
   before its quarantine ran.
2. **No DJs at 100% talk.** Three layers stopped every finished round:
   - **Playout sequencer.** It was in `linear` with 304 queued asks, so every
     round's start was pushed past its slot's deadline.
     **`/app/data/playout/mode` is now `shadow`**; writing `linear` back
     turns it on again.
   - **Silence rescue.** 53 of 55 rescue walks picked an advert, and the
     booth refused each one with no reason given.
   - **Live writing.** At 100% talk `dj_banter` refuses to write live while
     the larder is empty, so nothing filled the gap.
3. **Reply timing.** Lines waited for the feed's cards. `[reply-gap:buildup]`
   (2026-09-30) added each card's whole Rolodex to every pause. It also held a
   clip until its card had built plus a further 3 s.

## When the DJs are quiet

On the station host, after deploying: `sh tools/why-quiet.sh`. It prints the station's own diagnosis, the likeliest cause first with what to do, then what the roads said lately. The rules cover: radio off or paused, LINEAR playout with a backlog, a stalled clip, no voice engine ready, roads in back-off, how long since a DJ line aired, the rescue's last pass, the talk dial at 0, and changed speech gates. The same report is `GET /api/dj/why-quiet?text=1`.

## Diagnosing a conversation

Copy a key from the flowchart (the start pill or any line's code), then:

```bash
docker exec spark-agent python3 -c "import os,urllib.request as u;r=u.Request('http://127.0.0.1:8096/api/flow/KEY?text=1',headers={'Authorization':'Bearer '+os.environ['SPARK_AGENT_API_KEY']});print(u.urlopen(r).read().decode())"
```

The report lists every draw (its d100, path, winner and the candidates it
beat), every turn (what it was asked, what was said, its codes), its gates and
its banking steps.
