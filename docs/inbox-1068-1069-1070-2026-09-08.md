## 3. What changed tonight

**Loop starvation (#1070, the watchdog restarts)**
- `crystal_prompt_contracts(texts)` builds a round's source contracts and rhyme lookups in one worker
  thread; `crystal_tint`, the grouped first pass, the batched re-ask, the whole-round repair and
  `crystal_turn` use it. `crystal_rhyme_assistance` memoises its evidence per exact text, style pool and
  neural model for ten minutes (bounded to 512 entries). `_crystal_vocab()` keeps its union instead of
  rebuilding it twice per contract.
- `/api/orchestrator/rejections` and `/api/orchestrator/rejections/{id}` read the review store off the
  loop; the recovery loop does too. `LineReviewStore.summaries` now selects only the summary columns and
  lets SQLite pull the previews out of the JSON evidence instead of loading every row's complete context and
  evaluation documents.
- `RhymeAssistance._compact()` checkpoints and truncates the import's write-ahead log after `initialize()`
  and sets a 64 MB journal limit.
- System2's `refresh()`/`status()` deep-copy and `include_drafts` run in a worker thread; the status route
  serialises in one C call in a thread, straight from the live plans, and returns the bytes.
- **The second finding, after the switch:** dense py-spy sampling (60 samples at 0.4 s) showed a worker
  thread holding the GIL in 26 of them, mostly System2's own refresh: `include_drafts` decoded every
  candidate body of a kind once per slot (forty times a refresh, every 15 s) and `plan_hour` decodes them
  again; the C JSON decoder holds the GIL for each body, and with five to eight busy workers the air clock
  on the main thread lost most GIL contests - the 2-3 s freezes the pulse attributed to `load_settings`
  were that. Moving CPU-bound Python or JSON work to a thread does not free the loop; only doing less of it
  does. Now: `System2Store.candidates_by_slot` decodes the table once per hour of slots, the refresh runs
  every 60 s (plans lag a newly ready recording by at most a minute), and the status poll skips the deep
  copy. `plan_hour` still decodes the whole candidate table per planned hour; a decoded-row cache in the
  store, or pruning candidates absent from the inventory for more than a few hours, is the next step if
  the pulse still names it.
- The searxng configuration parse in `services_census` runs in a thread.

**Repetition and the cache (#1068)**
- `shelf_rest_now()`: the "raid the cupboard" judgment can shorten a repeat's rest to the one-hour floor
  (`SHELF_REUSE_REST_FLOOR`, 3600 s), never below it — applied in `shelf_take`, `shelf_repeat_ready` and
  `dialogue_stock_items`.
- `stock_expires_at(kind, row)` is the one expiry clock (unheard rows: the 24-hour burn horizon; repeats:
  their keep window; news: its story life). Rows are stamped `expires_at` when shelved or banked, expired
  rows are not stock in the common inventory (so the desk writes their replacement), and the System2
  candidate reads the same clock.
- `stock_used_by()` is written beside `aired_at` at every site that spends a row: the entry on air, its kind,
  the hour and the commitment.
- Emergency continuity: no line twice inside an hour (`CONTINUITY_REST_SECONDS`, 3600 s); when every
  recorded pair has gone out inside the hour the record carries the gap and the status says so; ten more
  pairs (fourteen in all); a line the crystal refuses is retried after ten minutes instead of blocking the
  pairs behind it. `/api/dj/continuity` reports `resting` and `rest_seconds`.

**Rhyme, crystal, tag cloud (#1068)**
- `/api/tint` carries `rhyme_assistance` (index ready, counts, vectors embedded, pending queries).
- `GET /api/crystals/{id}/rhymes?mind=&file=&start=&span=` reads the crystal as a rhyming dictionary: the
  word every chunk lands on, grouped by its CMUdict tail, counted per crystal / mind / song / passage of
  chunks; built in a thread and memoised ten minutes.
- 🎤 **Rhyme Cloud** in the 3JS list (panel `PINE_3JS`, desktop `THREEJS_VIEWS`): the word-cloud sphere

- `crystal_prompts._demand(strength)`: the style dial says what it means again ("all the way / firmly /
  lightly"), below the meaning-first priorities, in every shared crystal prompt.

**Scripts and explanations (#1069)**

## 4b. #1071 — the Gazette was not tinted

The 02:00 edition's own status line read "Crystal tint: 0/12 eligible paragraphs; 12 attempted; failed".
The edition record: 74 eligible paragraphs, 60 attempted (the press cap), 8 accepted, 575 s spent of the
600 s window, 52 paragraphs recorded as "rewrite not accepted" with no reason. `paper_tint_story` asked
`crystal_line` with no model, so each paragraph went to `tint_model_for` → gemma4:31b, whose two tint
permits belong to the dialogue (41 items waiting, deferrals at two a minute); the ask raised
`WritingDeferred`, the paper caught it as a generic exception, charged the attempt and moved on. Nine
seconds per attempt is the signature of a refusal, not a rewrite. Fixed: `crystal_line(..., model=)` is
passed through to the turn writer; the paper asks on the fast model's own tint lane
(`tint_fast_model()`, gemma4:e2b, a separate runner) with `kind="paper"`; a deferral is waited out up to
three times inside the window and never charged against the attempt caps; the paragraph record says
"deferred: …" or "the crystal refused the rewrite or stood down" instead of one blank phrase, and the
Gazette's status line counts the deferrals.

A press run on that build (edition `2026-09-08-03x0401`) confirmed the lane fix - not one deferral - and
showed the two remaining limits: one story at a time reached 11 of 84 eligible paragraphs before the
ten-minute window closed (about 50 s per paragraph: a fast-model ask plus the graded re-asks), and the
grader accepted 2 of the 11 (long prose paragraphs rewritten whole; the dialogue's own rate is about
85 % on short turns). Second change: the paper tints two stories at a time on the fast model's lane
(its admission is two per model), and under the hold the window is twenty minutes and 120 paragraphs
instead of ten and sixty. Acceptance is the crystal's grade, the same one the dialogue passes, and is
left as it is; if the paper's rate stays low the next lever is `room=2` in `paper_tint_story` (sentence
by sentence, chained), which raises the pass rate at the cost of more asks.

## 4c. #1077 · #1075 — no edition goes out untinted

The operator's rule: "I don't want the newspaper even making it without being tinted." The Ollama
runners each hold one parallel slot (`-np 1` on every llama-server), so a model answers one ask at a time
whatever the app allows; a whole edition (about 80 paragraphs at 15-50 s an ask) cannot be tinted inside a
ten- or twenty-minute window. Under the hold the press now keeps the edition until every eligible
paragraph is a bar or forty-five minutes are spent: accepted paragraphs are never asked again, a paragraph
refused whole is asked sentence by sentence (chained) on the next pass, up to eight passes, and what never
passes is cut before print (`paper_tint_cut`: a story keeps its first paragraph so nothing prints empty,
interview answers stay, classified notices that stayed plain are dropped, the records are re-keyed and the
status line counts the cut). This is the dialogue's own rule applied to the paper - rhyme or silence.
The next hourly press is skipped while a held edition is still on the press; the operator sees "pass N
over M stories still owed a rhyme" in the press steps.
