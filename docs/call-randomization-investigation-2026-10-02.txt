Approved implementation
See call-randomization-proposal-2026-10-02.json for approved option cues, weights, integration points and validation requirements.
- CALLOPEN1: 8 opening intents; requires a real pickup/invitation with fresh words, no name before introduction.
- CALLANGLE1: 10 ways to develop the same premise.
- CALLSTAKES1: 8 stakes lenses.
- CALLPROBE1: 8 grounded follow-up actions, quoting a brief anchor rather than the entire caller sentence.
All proposed table, category and option weights are 1.0. Their product is 5,120 combinations before existing reaction, arc and ending choices. This is a planning space, not evidence that the writer will realize 5,120 distinct calls.
- call_diversity_plan: draw and attach intents before the writer, through System Three and the Rolodex.
- call_diversity_repair: on a genuine collision, keep the premise and reroll the affected intent combination and downstream reactions/ending. Repair dependent replies coherently, validate the entire call, then bind its revision before recording.
- Draw retries uniformly among remaining valid combinations; record every exclusion. Persistent failures suggest additional distinct outcomes. Equal base weights must not silently receive state multipliers that make these wheels unequal.
- Use current writer-visit caps. Deferral preserves owed work and call identity; it should not consume a diversity retry or penalize an option.
- New options must have stable ids, provenance, validation and version history before they are eligible. Their creation, eligibility, draw and realized wording all need receipts visible from the Rolodex.

Room flow
Planning -> writing -> full-call validation -> recording -> cupboard admission -> broadcast must reference the same call id, node/turn ids and script revision. A pre-recording collision returns to the System Three recovery node. A writer deferral remains queued. Changed words invalidate their audio takes and dependent replies. A later stale-shelf collision sends affected work back for preparation rather than silently losing a required opening at the microphone. Monitor completion time and finished-audio throughput as well as rejection rate.

Local repair draws one fresh approved intent for each affected node within existing visit caps. The first whole-call similarity rewrite keeps call id, trace id, caller, premise and source while drawing a new plan (including independently rolled reactions and ending), increments the revision and refuses to replace a plan already bound to audio. Reservations expire after five minutes; the normal air-history check remains authoritative at broadcast. Empty drafts have their own failure reason.
